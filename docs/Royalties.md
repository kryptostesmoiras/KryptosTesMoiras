# Royalties — Kryptos Tēs Moiras

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the intended framework for royalties arising from the commercial distribution of applications through Kryptos Tēs Moiras.

Its purpose is to establish a transparent, auditable process for defining royalty terms, calculating amounts due, reporting transactions, and settling payments to the relevant rights holders.

This document is a general platform policy. The specific commercial agreement applicable to an application or publisher takes precedence where it expressly defines different terms.

## 2. Scope

These rules apply when Kryptos Tēs Moiras distributes a commercial application under an agreement that provides for royalties or a distribution commission.

They do not automatically apply to:

- free applications distributed without a royalty agreement;
- donations to the platform;
- domain registration or management subscriptions;
- AI functionality provided without a platform subscription;
- third-party products or services governed by separate terms.

Each application must have clearly defined distribution terms before commercial distribution begins.

## 3. Agreement Required

Before commercial distribution, the platform and the relevant rights holder or authorized publisher should establish a written agreement that identifies, as applicable:

- the application and its version or distribution channel;
- the parties and their respective roles;
- the royalty rate or calculation method;
- the revenue base used for calculation;
- the currency and payment method;
- the reporting period and payment schedule;
- how taxes, refunds, chargebacks, payment-processing fees, and other deductions are handled;
- the treatment of discounts, bundles, promotional access, and complimentary copies;
- the records available to the rights holder;
- the process for raising and resolving calculation disputes;
- the conditions for changing, suspending, or terminating distribution.

No royalty rate, payment frequency, or deduction is implied by this document unless it is specified in the applicable agreement.

## 4. Calculation Basis

The applicable agreement must define the royalty calculation basis. For example, it may use gross sales receipts or net eligible receipts after specifically identified deductions.

A calculation should be reproducible from transaction records. At minimum, the platform should record:

- transaction identifier and date;
- application or product identifier;
- transaction status;
- amount and currency;
- applicable discount;
- refund or chargeback, if any;
- permitted deductions under the agreement;
- royalty rate or calculation rule;
- calculated royalty amount;
- payment status and settlement reference.

The platform must not introduce undisclosed deductions or change the calculation method without following the applicable agreement.

## 5. Reports and Statements

For each reporting period defined by the agreement, the platform should provide a royalty statement containing, where relevant:

- the reporting period;
- eligible sales or transactions;
- gross amounts received;
- refunds and chargebacks;
- deductions permitted by the agreement;
- the calculation basis and applicable rate;
- the royalty amount accrued;
- adjustments from prior periods;
- amounts paid and amounts remaining payable.

Statements should be understandable and sufficiently detailed to allow the rights holder to verify the calculation, subject to appropriate privacy and security protections for customer information.

## 6. Payment and Settlement

Royalties should be paid according to the schedule and method specified in the applicable agreement.

A royalty is not considered settled merely because an internal ledger entry has been created. The platform should mark it as paid only after the relevant payment system or network confirms the transfer, and should retain a settlement reference where available.

If payment fails or is delayed, the platform should record the reason and follow the process specified in the agreement. Minimum payment thresholds, transaction fees, and handling of small balances must be disclosed in advance if they apply.

## 7. Refunds, Chargebacks, and Adjustments

The applicable agreement must state how refunds, chargebacks, cancelled orders, fraudulent transactions, and other reversals affect royalties.

Adjustments should be traceable to the original transaction and reporting period. Historical ledger entries should not be silently overwritten; corrections should be recorded as identifiable adjustment entries.

The platform should not deduct an amount from future royalties unless that treatment is permitted by the applicable agreement and applicable requirements.

## 8. Rights and Distribution Authorization

The party submitting an application for commercial distribution must have the rights and authority necessary to authorize the distribution and grant any permissions required by the applicable agreement.

The distributor should identify relevant third-party components and license conditions where those conditions affect commercial distribution. Acceptance of a royalty agreement does not transfer ownership of an application unless a separate written agreement expressly provides for that transfer.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the rights holder retains ownership of the application and grants only the distribution permissions expressly described in the applicable agreement.

## 9. Transparency and Records

Kryptos Tēs Moiras should maintain records sufficient to reconcile commercial transactions, accrued royalties, adjustments, and payments.

Access to financial records must be restricted to authorized parties. Reports should disclose only information necessary to verify the relevant royalties and should avoid exposing unrelated users' personal data, credentials, private repositories, or confidential business information.

The platform should define a reasonable period for retaining royalty records in accordance with its operational needs and applicable requirements.

## 10. Disputes and Corrections

A rights holder should be able to raise a documented question or dispute about a royalty statement through the designated contact channel.

The platform should review the relevant transaction records, explain the calculation, and correct any verified error through a traceable adjustment. The applicable agreement should define any notification deadline or dispute-resolution procedure.

## 11. Changes to Royalty Terms

Royalty terms for an existing application must not be changed unilaterally where the applicable agreement does not permit such a change.

Any change should be documented and communicated to the affected party before it takes effect. If agreement is required, the revised terms take effect only after the required acceptance.

## 12. Suspension or End of Distribution

The applicable agreement should specify when commercial distribution may be suspended or terminated, including cases such as a rights dispute, a security issue, a breach of agreed terms, or a request from an authorized rights holder.

Suspension or termination does not by itself erase royalties already accrued. Final statements and outstanding payments should be handled according to the agreement, subject to legitimate adjustments, disputes, or legal restrictions.

## 13. Relationship to Other Documents

This document should be read alongside:

- `FinancialModel.md`, which describes the platform's overall financial model;
- the applicable application distribution agreement;
- the platform's terms for publishers and users;
- any third-party license terms that apply to the distributed application.

If documents conflict, the applicable signed or accepted commercial agreement governs the royalty calculation to the extent of the conflict, unless mandatory requirements provide otherwise.

## 14. Status and Disclaimer

This document describes the intended royalty-management policy. It does not itself set a royalty rate, promise a minimum income, guarantee payment volume, or replace a specific commercial agreement.

Before commercial distribution begins, the platform should publish or execute the terms needed to make each application's royalty arrangement clear and verifiable.
