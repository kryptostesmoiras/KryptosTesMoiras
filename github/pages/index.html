01 — KRYPTOS

Designed for your independence.

Every language belongs here.

/ 01

Your language, your environment

Repositories accept any language. Static website hosting, builds and application execution are separate planned capabilities in isolated environments, without running projects on the platform host.

/ 02

Hashes, not your source

AI has no direct access to hosted source. A separate analyzer produces hashes and filtered CST-derived artifacts. Raw CSTs may contain code and must not be transmitted unchanged.

/ 03

Sentinel stays independent

The planned supervisor checks service health and containment conditions using deterministic rules. AI must not disable it, expand its own permissions or erase audit evidence.
